Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Overview

This notice explains how the Trinity Care Continuum website (trinitycarecontinuum.com) handles information. The website is informational. It does not have accounts, forms, or a patient portal, and it does not ask you to enter personal or health information.

What this website collects

  • We do not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics tools on this website.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may automatically record technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you visit. This information is handled under GitHub's privacy statement, not by Trinity Care Continuum.

Contacting us

You can reach our intake team by phone at (817) 910-6711, by email at intake@trinitymwc.com, or by fax at 800-915-0474.

Standard email is not a secure way to send health information. Please do not include a patient's health information in an email. For referrals, use fax or our online referral form, or call us and we will tell you how to proceed.

If you contact us, we use the information you give us to respond to you, and for the referral or care coordination you ask about.

Online referral form

The "Send a referral online" button opens a form hosted by Jotform, a separate service, on its own website. Information you submit through that form is handled through Jotform's service and our own handling of referrals, and is not collected by this website. Please use the form for referral purposes only.

Health information

Health information that we receive or create in connection with a referral or with care is handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including HIPAA where it applies. This website notice describes only the website and does not replace any notice of privacy practices that we provide to patients.

Links to other websites

This website links to other sites, such as Jotform. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites, so please review their notices.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Questions

For questions about this notice, call (817) 910-6711 or email intake@trinitymwc.com.

In a medical emergency, call 911. Do not use email, fax, or this website to report an emergency.
