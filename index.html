Our approach

Every patient deserves
a medical home.

Care can become fragmented when patients move between the hospital, skilled nursing, home health, and everyday life. Trinity helps connect the plan—so patients and families have support beyond a single visit or episode.

Our nurse practitioner-led model brings care management, clinical oversight, medication coordination, and patient education together around the needs of each person. When a primary care relationship exists, we strengthen it. When one is missing, we help establish appropriate access.

“Care does not stop when
the episode ends.”

Palliative care focuses on comfort, quality of life, and the goals that matter to the patient. It can be provided alongside treatment for serious illness and does not require a patient to be ready for hospice.

For patients & families

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Start here, or contact our team to discuss your situation.

Does Trinity work with my current doctor?

Yes. Our approach is to support existing provider relationships and coordinate with the patient's clinical team. If primary care access is missing, we help identify an appropriate path to care.

Is palliative care the same as hospice?

Palliative care supports quality of life during serious illness and can accompany active treatment. Hospice is a distinct model with specific eligibility requirements. Trinity helps patients and families navigate appropriate support as their needs change.

Can family members or caregivers be involved?

With the patient's permission, family members and caregivers can participate in care planning, education, and communication about the patient's needs and goals.

What will my program cost?

Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on the program, insurance benefits, and individual eligibility. Our team reviews available benefit information and discusses potential patient costs before enrollment.

Where does Trinity provide support?

Trinity delivers its programs by telehealth, so we can support patients anywhere in Texas, with no in-person visit required. Contact our team to confirm eligibility and next steps.
