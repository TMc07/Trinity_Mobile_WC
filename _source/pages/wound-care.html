What it is

Wound care that
comes to the patient.

Trinity Mobile Wound Care brings specialized wound care to patients where they live, so they do not have to travel for treatment. Their team serves nursing facilities, assisted living centers, and residential homes.

Trinity Care Continuum partners with them so that patients who need wound care can be connected to it alongside the support they receive from our team.

Visit Trinity Mobile Wound Care ↗

Wounds they treat

Advanced care for
complex wounds.

  • Diabetic wounds
  • Pressure injuries
  • Arterial wounds
  • Venous wounds
  • Surgical wounds
  • Traumatic injury wounds
Good to know

In person, through
our partner.

Wound care is provided in person by Trinity Mobile Wound Care and is separate from Trinity Care Continuum's telehealth programs. Please contact them directly to confirm service in your area, or call us and we will help point you in the right direction.

Coverage and out-of-pocket costs depend on insurance benefits and individual eligibility. Trinity Mobile Wound Care can review these with you.
